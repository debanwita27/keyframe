## Group 2 observations
- rhythm: three distinct grids recur — a hard 8f beat grid at 24fps (A_shipping-8-launch-videos, cuts at f8/16/24.../80), a 3f burst step inside conveyor "burst" segments (A_showreel-snippet, e.g. f85-87/88-90/100-102), and a 4f frame-quantized stutter with zero easing (B_galactic-motion-art's stutterHold, f34/38/42/46; B_dynamic-typography-d3's on-twos heldFrame). Logo pieces favor a 2f strobe square-wave for identity-flip cuts (B_logo-streetwear f100/102/104, B_logo-wip f367-382) rather than any beat-grid.
- structural: 6 of 12 are continuous-take (≤1 hard cut): B_chromatic-logo-2d3d (1 cut/257f), B_dynamic-typography-d3 (0/273f), B_editors-loop-3d-camera (0/323f), B_logo-aim (1/91f), B_logo-sage-library (0/179f), B_logo-streetwear (1/167f real cuts, 1 trailing artifact). The other 6 are cut-driven montages (5-21 cuts): A_shipping-8-launch-videos, A_showreel-snippet, A_sparkle-vertical, B_galactic-motion-art, B_logo-holo, B_logo-wip. Logo-anim pieces specifically split 4-continuous/3-cut-driven — brand marks lean toward the single unbroken take, event/process pieces (galactic, wip) lean toward hard cuts.
- gaps: none of these 12 show a pricing/plan beat, a testimonial or customer-logo wall, a before/after comparison, a multi-device (phone+desktop) responsive framing, or a real signup/download CTA with a URL or button — every "cta" act in this group closes on a brand mark, a wordmark lockup, or a UI screenshot, never an explicit action prompt. Also absent: any voiceover/dialogue-timed cut (all rhythm is beat-grid or camera-driven, not speech-driven), and any live cursor-drag/scrub interaction (B_editors-loop-3d-camera's marquee and A_connect-to-figma-style click proofs are absent here — only a static selection-marquee, no drag).

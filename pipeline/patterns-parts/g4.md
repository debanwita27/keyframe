## Group 4 observations
- rhythm: two extremes recur — very long unbroken takes (438f/7.3s single shot in new-this-week; 330f curve-editor dwell in you-dont-always-need-to-start-from-scratch) vs. dense multi-cut reels (jitter-2024: 43 cuts/1000f, median 20f, one 17f strobe-whip cluster at f208-224; after-4-years: 13 cuts/464f, median 33f, one 7f cut). Sub-11f "flash cluster" beats (7-8f, always in groups of exactly 3) recur across mthblt_B specs as a deliberate density device, not a detector error.
- structural: only 2 of 12 are continuous-take (<=1 hard cut): sound-on-logo-animation (1 cut, 178f) and letter-spacing-animations (0 cuts, 120f). The other 10 are cut-driven multi-shot edits, several extremely so (jitter-2024 43 cuts, you-dont-always 13 cuts over 67.7s, fifa-logo-animation 10 cuts over 52.7s).
- featuredemo: the two jittervideo_A specs (and to a lesser extent recreating-fifa/you-dont-always in group B) do something no other reference in the corpus does — they screen-record software literally obeying a typed natural-language instruction, with a visible intermediate reasoning trace ("Thinking...", step list, "Done — ...") before the canvas commits, then bind the canvas's resulting state change to the same frame the trace completes. Magic Resize additionally reflows one content model across 5 aspect-ratio artboards from a single shared frame counter — a genuine multi-output state change, not an implied cut. No byshubh-era spec shows a tool being commanded; they only show finished UI states.
- gaps: none of these 12 show a real pricing/CTA/URL end-card (most end on a brand-lockup pill, a mid-gesture drag, or a still-looping poster); none sustain a long-form pacing arc (the longest, you-dont-always at 67.7s, is still one repeating tutorial unit, not a rising act structure); none cut between two or more real talking-head/testimonial clips (only single-presenter tutorials); none show true fluid/cloth/soft-body sim beyond a seeded splash-particle burst; the two "bulk/localization" beats (grid-multiply-localization-reveal, bulk-variant-tile-wall) fake unique per-item content via placeholder reuse, so neither is a real answer to genuine multi-language/accessibility copy reflow.
